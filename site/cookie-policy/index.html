Cookie Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. This page explains the cookies and similar browser storage this website uses.

What we use

NameTypePurposeHow long
sc-consentBrowser storage (strictly necessary)Remembers your cookie choice so the banner is not shown on every page.12 months
__Host-sc_sessionCookie (strictly necessary)Keeps authorised Skills College staff signed in to the website management area. It is never set for other visitors.8 hours

This website does not currently use advertising, social media or analytics cookies, and it does not track you across other websites.

Optional cookies

If we add website statistics in future, they will only run after you choose Accept all in the cookie banner. You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link in the footer of every page.

Controlling cookies in your browser

Most browsers let you see, block and delete cookies in their settings. Blocking strictly necessary storage may mean the website asks for your cookie choice again on each visit.

More information

Read our Privacy Policy for how we handle personal information, or contact us at sales@skillscollege.co.za.
