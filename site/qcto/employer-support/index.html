QCTO Support for Employers and Consultants

Turn QCTO requirements into a workable programme

Skills College helps employers and consultants plan qualification fit, accreditation, workplace components, learner requirements, timelines, assessment and reporting so implementation is practical from the start.

Talk to a QCTO specialist →

When to talk to us

  • You are moving from a legacy SETA qualification to a QCTO occupational qualification.
  • You need help selecting an occupational qualification aligned to a role or workforce need.
  • You need to understand workplace experience requirements.
  • You need guidance on EISA or assessment planning.
  • You want one partner to coordinate programme implementation and reporting.

What Skills College can help with

  • Qualification fit and current accreditation confirmation
  • Programme implementation planning
  • Learner recruitment and onboarding
  • Workplace readiness and work experience planning
  • Training and learner support
  • Assessment coordination
  • Progress reporting and close-out evidence

Our approach

  1. 01

    Understand

    Clarify the role, business need and current programme position.

  2. 02

    Map

    Identify the qualification route and implementation requirements.

  3. 03

    Prepare

    Plan learners, workplace components, documentation and assessment.

  4. 04

    Implement

    Coordinate training, workplace learning and learner support.

  5. 05

    Monitor

    Track progress, reporting and assessment readiness.
