QCTO curriculum

How a QCTO occupational curriculum works

A QCTO occupational curriculum is built around an occupational profile and the tasks required in that role. The learning journey normally combines three core components.

View QCTO qualifications →
  1. 01

    Knowledge Modules

    The theory and underpinning knowledge a learner needs for the occupation.

  2. 02

    Practical Skills Modules

    Structured practice that develops the skills required to perform occupational tasks.

  3. 03

    Work Experience Modules

    Supervised workplace exposure that allows the learner to apply knowledge and skills in a real working environment.

Bringing it together

Together, these components prepare the learner for competent performance and the required external assessment process.

Knowledge Modules+Practical Skills Modules+Work Experience Modules
