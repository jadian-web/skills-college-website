QCTO explained

What is the QCTO?

The Quality Council for Trades and Occupations, or QCTO, is responsible for occupational qualifications within South Africa's National Qualifications Framework. Its role includes the design, implementation, assessment, quality assurance and certification of occupational qualifications.

Explore QCTO solutions →

In simple terms

QCTO qualifications are built around occupations and the work a person must be able to perform. They bring together theoretical knowledge, practical skills and structured workplace experience.

What this means for employers

  • Choose qualifications that align to real occupational roles.
  • Use an appropriately accredited training provider.
  • Plan for the practical and workplace experience components.
  • Prepare learners for the External Integrated Summative Assessment where required.
  • Build programme timelines, workplace and assessment requirements into the implementation plan from the start.
