The QCTO transition

From legacy SETA qualifications to the QCTO framework

South Africa's occupational training landscape has moved from legacy SETA qualifications toward QCTO occupational qualifications. For employers, the practical impact is that programme selection, provider accreditation, workplace experience and external assessment must be planned within the current QCTO framework.

Talk to a QCTO specialist →

What changes in practice

  • Occupational qualifications are structured around real job roles and occupational tasks.
  • Knowledge, practical skills and workplace experience are distinct parts of the curriculum.
  • Providers and assessment centres must hold the relevant current accreditation.
  • External assessment requirements need to be understood early in the learner journey.
  • Employers may need to prepare or register workplaces for work experience components.

Planning an existing programme?

When you enquire, share the qualification title and ID, learner registration status, current provider, proposed workplace arrangement and any assessment requirements. Our team can help identify the next steps for your programme.
