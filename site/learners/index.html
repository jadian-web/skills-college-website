How to apply

Follow these steps for any opportunity listed on this website.

  1. 01

    Find an opportunity

    Browse current opportunities and open the one that suits you.

  2. 02

    Check the requirements

    Read who can apply, the closing date and the location before you start.

  3. 03

    Prepare your documents

    Get the documents listed on the opportunity ready, for example your ID, CV and latest qualification.

  4. 04

    Apply

    Use the apply button or email address on the opportunity. Apply before the closing date.

  5. 05

    Selection

    Applications are checked against the programme criteria. Shortlisted applicants are contacted using the details they provide.

Stay safe when applying

Skills College does not ask for money to apply for a learnership, internship or bursary. If anyone asks you to pay to apply or to secure a place, do not pay and report it to our recruitment team.

Learner FAQs

Do I need an account to apply?

No. You can browse opportunities and read the requirements without an account. Follow the application instructions on each opportunity. The Student Portal is for learners who are already enrolled.

Is there a fee to apply?

No. Skills College does not charge learners a fee to apply for an opportunity.

What is a stipend?

A stipend is a monthly allowance paid to learners on some programmes. Whether a stipend is paid, and how much, depends on the programme and is shown on the opportunity where it applies.

How will I know if I have been selected?

Shortlisted applicants are contacted using the phone number or email address they give in their application, so make sure your details are correct.

There are no opportunities open. What can I do?

New opportunities are added as soon as they open, so check back regularly. You can also contact our recruitment team to ask about upcoming intakes.

Where does training take place?

Training locations are listed on each opportunity. Our training campus is in Soshanguve and our head office is in Irene, Centurion.

Talk to our recruitment team

For questions about an opportunity or an application, contact the recruitment team. Current students can sign in to the Student Portal.
