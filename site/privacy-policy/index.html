Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Skills College respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and our services, why we collect it, how we protect it and the rights you have under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

Who we are

Skills College is the responsible party for personal information collected through this website. You can contact us at:

  • Skills College
  • 33 Sovereign Drive, Route 21 Corporate Park, Irene, Centurion
  • Phone: 012 541 0100
  • Email: sales@skillscollege.co.za

Questions about this policy or your personal information can be sent to our Information Officer at the email address above, marked For the attention of the Information Officer.

Information we collect

We only collect information that we need. Depending on how you use our website and services, this may include:

  • Enquiry details you enter in our contact form: your name, company, email address, phone number, the service you are interested in and your message.
  • B-BBEE scorecard calculator details: your name, company, job title, contact details, sector, SETA, turnover and the scorecard figures you choose to enter, together with the calculated result.
  • Learner and applicant information when you apply for a learnership, bursary or programme (collected through our learner portal and recruitment process), which may include identity, education, employment, disability and demographic information needed for B-BBEE and SETA reporting.
  • Technical information that our hosting provider records automatically for security, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request.

Some learner information, such as race and disability status, is special personal information under POPIA. We process it only where the law allows it, for example to meet B-BBEE, Employment Equity and SETA reporting requirements, and with your consent where consent is required.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide the services you ask for.
  • To prepare and explain your indicative B-BBEE scorecard result and follow up with you about it.
  • To recruit, enrol, support and report on learners in Skills Development programmes.
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations, including reporting to SETAs, the QCTO and B-BBEE verification agencies.
  • To keep our website secure, prevent spam and abuse, and fix technical problems.

We will not use your information for direct marketing by electronic communication unless you have given consent or you are an existing client, and every marketing message will include a way to opt out.

Why we are allowed to process it

We process personal information on one or more of these grounds under section 11 of POPIA: your consent; to take steps you have requested or to perform a contract with you; to comply with an obligation imposed by law; or to pursue our legitimate interests or those of a third party to whom the information is supplied, in a way that does not unreasonably affect your rights.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only where necessary with:

  • Employers and clients for whom we manage a programme you are part of.
  • SETAs, the QCTO, assessment bodies and B-BBEE verification agencies, where reporting is required.
  • Service providers who help us run our business, such as our website host (Netlify), email and IT providers, under written agreements that require them to protect your information.
  • Authorities, where we are required to by law.

Information stored outside South Africa

Our website is hosted by Netlify, and some of our IT providers store information on servers outside South Africa, for example in the United States or the European Union. Where information is transferred across borders, we make sure it is protected by laws, binding agreements or corporate rules that give a level of protection substantially similar to POPIA, as required by section 72.

How we protect it

  • All website traffic is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Access to our systems is limited to staff who need it, protected by strong passwords stored only as secure hashes, and sign-in attempts are rate limited.
  • Our forms include spam protection, and the information you submit is stored with providers that encrypt data at rest.

If we have reasonable grounds to believe that your personal information has been accessed by an unauthorised person, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as required by section 22 of POPIA.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected, or for as long as the law requires. Enquiries and scorecard results are kept for up to three years after our last contact with you. Learner records are kept for the periods required by SETA, QCTO and tax legislation. After that, information is deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

Under POPIA you have the right to:

  • Ask whether we hold personal information about you and request a copy of it.
  • Ask us to correct or delete information that is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, excessive or unlawfully obtained.
  • Object to the processing of your information on reasonable grounds, and object to direct marketing at any time.
  • Withdraw consent you have given, without affecting processing that took place before.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator.

To make a request, email us at sales@skillscollege.co.za. We may ask you to confirm your identity first. Requests for records can also be made under our PAIA Manual.

The Information Regulator

If you are not satisfied with how we handle your personal information, you may complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa). Contact details are available on its website: inforegulator.org.za.

Cookies

This website uses only cookies and browser storage that are needed for it to work. Read our Cookie Policy for details and to change your choices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. Significant changes will be highlighted on this website.
