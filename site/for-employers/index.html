YES programme

Support youth employment through initiatives that create meaningful workplace opportunities and help young people gain valuable work experience.

The Youth Employment Service (YES) is a national, business-led initiative. Businesses that create 12-month work experience opportunities for unemployed black youth and meet the YES targets can be recognised with up to two additional B-BBEE levels. We can help you plan placements, recruit youth and manage their support.

How to get started

A simple, no-obligation first step.

  1. 01

    Book a consultation

    Tell us about your organisation, your skills development goals and any B-BBEE targets you are working towards.

  2. 02

    Scoping call

    We review your headcount, payroll, current programmes and timelines, and the outcomes you need.

  3. 03

    Proposal

    You receive a recommended solution with programme options, timelines, roles and an estimated budget.

  4. 04

    Implementation

    Once approved, we recruit, enrol and support learners, and report to you throughout the programme.
