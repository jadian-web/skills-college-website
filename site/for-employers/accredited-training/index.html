Accredited Training

Accredited training built around workplace needs

Access accredited training programmes and occupational qualifications that meet your organisation's skills development needs.

Talk to a training specialist →

How we deliver

  • Customised learning solutions based on employer needs.
  • Blended delivery combining face-to-face and digital learning where appropriate.
  • Qualified subject matter experts, facilitators, assessors and moderators.
  • Practical learning linked to workplace application.
  • Project management and learner support throughout delivery.

Find the right qualification or skills programme

Tell us about the role, skills need or programme you are planning. Our team will help identify suitable training options and confirm the relevant qualification details and accreditation scope.

Search the qualification directory →Get qualification guidance →

How training enrolment works

  1. 01

    Training needs

    Tell us the roles, number of learners, locations and timelines.

  2. 02

    Programme match

    We recommend qualifications or skills programmes within our accreditation scope.

  3. 03

    Enrolment

    Learner documents are collected and learners are registered with the relevant quality partner.

  4. 04

    Delivery and assessment

    Training, assessment and moderation are delivered, with progress reports to you.

  5. 05

    Certification

    Results are submitted for certification, and you receive close-out documentation.
