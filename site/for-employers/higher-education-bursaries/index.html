Higher Education Bursaries

Higher education bursaries managed from recruitment to reporting

Manage bursary programmes from learner recruitment and selection through to ongoing support and reporting.

Discuss a bursary programme →

Why employers use the service

  • Flexible programme timing aligned to business needs.
  • Learner recruitment and screening support.
  • Programme structures for employees, employee dependants or unemployed learners, where appropriate.
  • Study fields can be aligned to the employer's industry and skills priorities.
  • Support with learner documentation, contracts and registration.
  • Ongoing learner support and programme administration.
  • Compilation of supporting documentation for employer reporting and verification purposes.

Bursary details

The exact criteria, amounts and dates are agreed with you for each programme.

Who may be funded

Your employees, employees' dependants or unemployed learners, studying at an accredited public or registered private higher education institution. Criteria such as field of study, academic results and household income are agreed with you.

Eligible expenses

Typically tuition and registration fees. Where you choose to fund them, approved costs beyond tuition, such as accommodation, learning materials, transport and meals, can be added through Ignite Bursaries.

Documents learners provide

Certified ID, latest academic results, proof of application or registration at the institution, and proof of household income where the criteria require it.

Indicative timelines

Recruitment and selection usually run in the months before the academic year starts, so we recommend planning at least three months ahead. Payments follow the institution's registration and fee dates.

Reports you receive

Signed bursary agreements, proof of registration and payments, academic results each term or semester, and close-out documents to support your SETA and B-BBEE evidence.

Higher Education Bursaries or Ignite?

Higher Education Bursaries is our full bursary management service, usually covering tuition. Ignite Bursaries is a separate programme for approved education-related costs beyond tuition. You can use either one, or both together.

What Skills College manages

  • Programme design and project management
  • Recruitment aligned to approved criteria
  • Applicant communication and screening
  • Registration support
  • Document collection and contracting
  • Learner support during the programme
  • Reporting and evidence management
  • Access to learner digital tools where applicable

Featured programme

Ignite Bursaries

Invest in education. Support the learner. Ignite the future.

Help learners cover costs beyond tuition

A flexible, managed bursary solution that enables employers to support qualifying tertiary students with approved education-related expenses beyond tuition fees.

Support can include:

  • Student accommodation
  • Learning materials and study resources
  • Transport assistance
  • Meals and basic learner support
  • Registration-related costs
  • Other approved tertiary education expenses

Skills College can support candidate sourcing and recruitment, screening and selection, learner onboarding, monitoring and mentoring, attendance and performance tracking, progress reporting, compliance administration, supporting documentation and programme close-out.

The bursary process

  1. 01

    Agree criteria

    Study fields, institutions, number of bursaries, budget and selection criteria are agreed with you.

  2. 02

    Recruit and select

    We advertise, screen applications and documents, and present a shortlist for your approval.

  3. 03

    Contract and register

    Bursary agreements are signed and registration with the institution is confirmed.

  4. 04

    Support and monitor

    Learners receive ongoing support while we track results and attendance each term or semester.

  5. 05

    Report and close out

    You receive regular progress reports and the documents needed for SETA and B-BBEE evidence.
