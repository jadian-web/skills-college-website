Ignite Bursaries

Igniting Access. Supporting Education. Building Futures.

Ignite Bursaries is a bursary funding solution developed by Skills College to help businesses maximise the impact of their bursary spend while supporting South African learners in accessing and completing tertiary education.

Discuss Ignite Bursaries →

A smarter approach to bursary spend

The cost of tertiary education goes beyond tuition fees. For many students, accommodation, transport, learning materials and basic living costs can create significant barriers to completing their studies. Ignite Bursaries is designed to help bridge that gap through a structured, managed funding solution.

How Ignite Bursaries works

Skills College helps employers structure and administer bursary support for qualifying tertiary learners. Funding arrangements and approved cost categories are confirmed as part of the programme. Support can include:

  • Student accommodation
  • Learning materials and study resources
  • Transport assistance
  • Meals and basic learner support
  • Registration-related costs
  • Other approved tertiary education expenses

The objective is to give learners the additional support they need to remain engaged in their studies and progress towards completion.

Why Ignite Bursaries?

For employers and consultants

  • Flexibility: Structure bursary funding around available budget and approved programme requirements.
  • Impact: Support learners with the real costs associated with tertiary education.
  • Simplicity: Access a managed solution rather than administering every aspect of the bursary programme internally.
  • Visibility: Receive structured reporting on how the bursary contribution is being utilised.
  • Transformation: Turn bursary spend into a meaningful investment in education, skills and future talent.

For learners

  • Access: Financial assistance can help make tertiary education more accessible.
  • Support: Assistance can extend beyond tuition to essential education-related expenses.
  • Continuity: Additional financial support can help learners remain focused on their studies.
  • Opportunity: Successful completion of tertiary education can create pathways towards further Skills Development and employment.

Managed administration

Skills College provides a structured framework for managing the learner journey and associated administration. Support can include:

  • Candidate sourcing and recruitment
  • Candidate screening and selection
  • Learner onboarding
  • Learner monitoring and mentoring
  • Attendance and performance tracking
  • Progress reporting
  • Compliance administration
  • Supporting documentation and programme close-out

The Ignite difference

Traditional bursary funding can focus primarily on paying for education. Ignite Bursaries focuses on supporting the learner. By combining funding with structured administration, monitoring, reporting and learner support, Ignite Bursaries creates a more comprehensive approach to bursary investment.

Education+Accommodation+Essential Support+Monitoring+Opportunity

Invest in education. Support the learner. Ignite the future.

Every learner supported represents more than a bursary. It represents an opportunity to gain an education, develop skills, complete a qualification and participate meaningfully in the economy.
