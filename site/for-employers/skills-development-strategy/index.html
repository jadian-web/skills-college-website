What we help you plan

Skills College translates skills development requirements into practical programmes that help employers develop their workforce and measure the results of their programmes.

  1. Skills development spend and programme mix
  2. Learnerships, internships and workplace experience
  3. Higher education bursaries
  4. Skills development for people with disabilities
  5. QCTO occupational qualifications
  6. Employed and unemployed learner strategies
  7. Absorption and progression planning
  8. Budget, timing and project milestones
  9. Evidence, reporting and close-out requirements

Our approach

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    Understand the business, scorecard priorities and workforce needs.

  2. 02

    Design

    Build a tailored programme mix and implementation plan.

  3. 03

    Implement

    Coordinate learners, training, workplaces, administration and delivery.

  4. 04

    Measure

    Monitor progress, report and prepare evidence for close-out.

From planning to implementation

  • Strategic consultation and scenario planning aligned to available budget
  • Detailed project implementation and learner management
  • Financial oversight and controlled spend allocation
  • Ongoing monitoring, compliance management and reporting

Your first consultation

A focused conversation, usually 45 to 60 minutes, online or in person.

What it covers

  • Your skills development and B-BBEE objectives
  • Current programmes, spend and timelines
  • Learner, workplace and absorption options
  • Funding, SETA and QCTO considerations

What to bring

  • Your skills development budget, or your annual payroll (leviable amount) if the budget is not set yet
  • Your workforce priorities: the roles, skills gaps or teams you want to develop
  • Your sector, and your latest B-BBEE certificate or affidavit if you have one
  • Planning dates, such as your financial year-end and B-BBEE verification date
  • Your Workplace Skills Plan (WSP) and Annual Training Report (ATR), if available

What you receive

  • A written summary of the options discussed
  • An agreed programme plan or implementation roadmap, with timelines
  • An estimated budget and learner numbers
  • Clear next steps and who is responsible for each

Example deliverables, such as a sample programme plan or progress report, can be shared during the consultation.
