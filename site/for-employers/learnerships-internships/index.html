Learnerships & Internships

Managed learnerships and internships for workplace-ready talent

Skills College manages employed and unemployed learnerships and internships from programme selection and learner onboarding through to training, workplace experience, reporting and progression.

Request a consultation →

Who this is for

  • Employers implementing employed or unemployed learnerships.
  • Businesses that need help recruiting suitable learners.
  • Employers that need host workplace placement for practical experience.
  • Companies looking for structured internship management and workplace readiness support.
  • Organisations that want one partner to manage programme administration and reporting.
  • Consultants coordinating Skills Development programmes for clients.

What Skills College manages

  • Programme and qualification selection
  • Learner recruitment and screening
  • Onboarding, documentation and registration
  • Theory and blended learning
  • Host employer placement where required
  • Workplace experience support
  • Assessment and moderation
  • Learner support and administration
  • Monthly reporting
  • Close-out and absorption support

Employed or unemployed?

Unemployed learnerships

Skills College can support recruitment, training and workplace placement where the employer cannot accommodate learners directly.

Employed learnerships

Training is structured around existing employees and the employer's operational needs, with a focus on practical implementation and minimal disruption.

Internship management

Help interns enter the workplace with structure and support

Skills College can manage internship programmes that support workplace readiness, professional conduct, accountability and progression into meaningful employment.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between an employed and unemployed learnership?

An employed learnership develops existing employees. An unemployed learnership recruits learners who are not employed when the programme begins. Skills College helps plan recruitment, training and workplace experience around the agreed programme.

Can Skills College recruit learners for us?

Yes. Skills College supports learner sourcing, screening, onboarding and documentation against the agreed programme criteria.

Can Skills College provide host workplace placement?

Skills College can support host employer placement where required. The workplace arrangement and available capacity are confirmed during programme planning.

How is workplace experience managed?

Workplace experience is planned with the employer or approved host and linked to the programme requirements. Skills College supports the learner journey, administration and progress reporting.

What reporting will the employer receive?

Skills College provides monthly progress reporting and manages supporting documentation and evidence for programme close-out.

Can Skills College support absorption after completion?

Yes. Skills College can help plan learner progression and absorption from the start of the programme. The employment route depends on programme requirements and suitable opportunities.
