About Skills College

Teaching skills to change lives

Skills College is a specialist Skills Development partner that combines compliant programme design with practical learning, learner support and measurable outcomes for employers and learners.

Book a consultation →View accreditations →

Who we are

Established in 2008, Skills College works alongside employers to manage the full Skills Development lifecycle, from strategy and learner recruitment to accredited training, workplace experience, assessment, reporting and progression.

Why we exist

Skills College exists to teach practical skills that change lives. We create meaningful access to Skills Development while helping employers meet their Skills Development and B-BBEE objectives in a compliant and sustainable way.

Our mission

To teach skills that change lives and to empower companies to create meaningful, compliant and sustainable development opportunities.

Our vision

To be a leading Skills Development partner recognised for innovation, reliability and measurable impact.

Our values

  • Accountability
  • Innovative
  • Integrity
  • Service Excellence
  • Sustainable Impact

What we do

Skills College brings together strategy, project management, learner recruitment, accredited training, workplace activation, quality assurance, reporting and progression support.

How learning is delivered

Learning solutions can combine face-to-face delivery, digital learning and structured workplace experience depending on the qualification, programme and client requirements.

People and expertise

The Skills College delivery team includes consultants, managers, education specialists, administrators, facilitators, assessors and moderators who support programmes from planning through to completion.
