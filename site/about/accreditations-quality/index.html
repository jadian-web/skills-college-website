Our quality assurance process

  1. 01

    Recruitment

    Source and screen learners against the agreed programme criteria.

  2. 02

    Registration

    Collect and validate the documentation required for the programme and registration process.

  3. 03

    Theoretical learning

    Deliver accredited knowledge components and learner support.

  4. 04

    Workplace activation

    Support practical learning and workplace experience with the employer or approved host.

  5. 05

    Assessment & moderation

    Complete assessment and moderation in line with programme and quality body requirements.

  6. 06

    Reporting & close-out

    Track progress, compile evidence and support programme completion.
